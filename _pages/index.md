I keep two kinds of notebooks here. One is for **research** — things I find
interesting in machine learning and dynamical systems, written out until I
actually understand them. The other is for **cinema** — reviews, longer
impressions of movements and directors, and, eventually, my own work.

Different subjects, the same impulse: to look closely at something and try to
say what is really going on.
